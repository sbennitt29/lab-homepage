Games I Went To!

My personal experience at the 2026 World Cup

I live in the San Francisco Bay Area, and I was able to attend two matches throughout the tournament at Levi's Stadium.

Qatar vs. Switzerland

This was the first World Cup game I've ever attended, and it did not disappoint. Qatar was a huge underdog, but after going down 1-0, they managed to tie the game in the 94th minute, right in front of our seats!

USA vs. Bosnia and Herzegovina

I had to beg my dad to get tickets to this match, and because it was the USA, he agreed. It was an incredible atmosphere and a fun game, as we saw the USA defeat Bosnia and Herzegovina 2-0 to move on to the round of 16.
